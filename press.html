Voice & tone

How we write.

eventech writes in plain English, short sentences, and concrete claims. We say what we do, what we don't, and what it costs. We avoid corporate jargon, fake metrics, and breathless superlatives. We use "we" and "I" interchangeably because the founder is the engineer.

Approved descriptions: "AI & automation studio", "San Francisco AI studio", "senior-only AI delivery".

Use with care: "AI agency" implies middlemen and overhead — we are not an agency.

Avoid: "AI consulting firm", "AI transformation partner", "world-class", "best-in-class", "industry-leading", "Fortune 500 clients" (we don't have any), and any superlative without a source.
