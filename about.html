The story

Most AI projects die in pilots. We ship them in production.

eventech is a focused AI & automation studio. We help companies automate operations, launch AI-native products, and build custom AI systems — in weeks, not quarters. We were founded in 2026 with a single belief: that the next decade of business software will be built with AI at the center, and the people who ship it will be the ones who matter.

Before eventech, our founder spent over a decade building production AI for early-stage startups, post-seed scale-ups, and a few enterprise teams in between. The same patterns kept repeating: a brief that promises everything, a six-month integration that delivers a slide deck, and a pilot that never quite graduates to production. eventech exists to break that loop — by being small, senior, and ruthlessly focused on shipping.

How we work

From brief to production in four steps.

01

Audit

30 minutes on a call. We map your workflow, identify where AI pays off, and scope the highest-ROI starting point. If AI isn't the answer, we'll say so.

02

Design

A fixed-price quote within 48 hours. Plain English, no decks, no retainers. You know exactly what you're paying for and what you'll get.

03

Ship

Working software in week one. Real data, real users, real quick. Most projects ship in 2–6 weeks, end-to-end.

04

Operate

Optional monthly care. We tune evals, monitor quality, ship improvements. Month-to-month, cancel any time.

What we believe

Three principles, no exceptions.

/01

You work with the founder.

Every brief is read by Sofiane. Every line of code is written by Sofiane. No account managers, no offshore handoffs, no second-rate juniors. When you hire eventech, you hire the person on the call.

/02

Shipped, not pitched.

We measure success on systems in production on your real data — not on slides in a Notion or pilots that never graduate. Every engagement ends with a working system you own and can defend.

/03

Auditable end-to-end.

Every model call, retrieval, and tool invocation is logged. You can explain every decision your AI makes — to your team, your users, your auditor, or your regulator. No black boxes.
