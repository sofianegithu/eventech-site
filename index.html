03 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

The things prospective clients ask us most. If yours isn't here, send us a note.

What does eventech do?

eventech is an AI & automation studio that helps companies automate operations, launch AI-native products, and build custom AI systems. Every engagement is senior-only (no account managers, no offshore handoffs) and ships in 2–6 weeks under a fixed scope.

How long does an eventech project take?

Most projects ship in 2–6 weeks. We deliver working software in week one against your real data, then iterate. The average ship time across all engagements is 6 weeks.

How much does eventech cost?

We work on fixed scope, not retainers. You get a fixed-price quote within 48 hours of an initial 30-minute audit call. Monthly care is optional and cancel-anytime.

Who works on eventech projects?

Every brief is read, designed, and built by Sofiane Moudjahed — the founder. You work directly with the person doing the work; there are no account managers, no offshore teams, and no second-rate juniors.

Where is eventech based?

eventech is based in San Francisco, California and works with companies in the US, EU, and MENA. Engagements are remote-first.

What is eventech's refund policy?

Full refund is available within a 72-hour cooling-off period, provided no substantive work has commenced. After that, refunds are pro-rated to work actually performed. See the full Refund Policy for details.
