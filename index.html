02 / How we work

From whiteboard to production in four steps.

01

Diagnose

Two weeks, one senior team. We map the workflows, systems, and data we'd need to touch — and what we'd build first.

Design

A small squad (designer, engineer, researcher) prototypes the system against your real data. You see working software in week three.

03

Deploy

Scale

Our platform team keeps watch, tunes evals, and ships improvements. You focus on the business; the system gets sharper every week.
